Game Cabinet Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Game Cabinet is made by 12 Acre Films in Spokane, Washington.

What we collect

Nothing. Game Cabinet does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your device

Your best scores and game progress are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Device motion

One game, Gap Escape, uses your device's motion sensors to steer the ball. That data is used in the moment to play the game and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Game Cabinet does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
